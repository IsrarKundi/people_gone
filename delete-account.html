Delete Your Uncrowd Account and Data

Uncrowd is published by Artivo.

Most of your data already deletes itself

  • Photos you upload for processing, and the cleaned results, are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours. You do not need to request this.
  • Photos saved to your phone are yours — deleting them from your phone removes them completely.

What else exists, and how to delete it

Uncrowd uses an anonymous account (no name, email, or password) to count your monthly free saves and any Pro entitlement. This account record, its save counts, and its job history can be permanently deleted on request.

  1. Open Uncrowd on your phone.
  2. Send us an email from any email address to israrkundi37@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Uncrowd data". If possible, send it right after using the app once, and tell us the approximate date and time — this helps us locate the correct anonymous record, since it has no name or email attached to it.
  3. We will delete the anonymous account record, its save counts, and its job history, and confirm by reply email once done. Requests are completed within 30 days, normally much sooner.

What is kept after deletion

After deletion, no photos, account record, or usage history remain on our servers. Records that Google Play keeps about purchases (for example a subscription receipt) are held by Google under Google's own policies and are not controlled by us.
