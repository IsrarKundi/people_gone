- **2. 02_sky_meadow.jpg** — Five walkers cross the field instead of two; keepers are designated as the front pair. A farmhouse and trees sit on the horizon rather than a bare sky/grass horizon.
- **3. 03_plain_wall_portrait.jpg** — STRONG DEVIATION — No stock photo matching "solo portrait against a wall plus a photobomber" could be found on Pexels/Unsplash. This photo shows a lone man standing on a path beside a massive historic stone fortress wall outdoors. There is NO second person / photobomber in the frame, so this row has no removal target; it can only serve as a keeper-protection control (a correct pipeline output is an unchanged photo). Recommend replacing with a real user photo before running the gate.
- **4. 04_desert_dune.jpg** — All four figures are silhouetted walking along the dune ridge at sunset; the keeper is the lead figure and the other three are the removal targets (the manifest imagined tourists in a valley below the subject).
- **5. 05_coastal_cliff.jpg** — STRONG DEVIATION — The viewpoint is a mountain summit above a sea of clouds at sunrise, not a coastal cliff over the sea. Subjects are a standing camping group (~10 people, tent and motorbikes nearby); keepers are designated as the pair at the left of the group, and ~8 people are removable instead of 2 on a trail.
- **6. 06_park_lawn.jpg** — Subjects are three adults reclining on a picnic blanket in an open grass field, not a child playing; the removal targets are the two companions on the same blanket, not distant picnickers.
