## Phase 1 — Fix before launch (Gemini's FACT items)

**G1.1 Photo container consistency [CORRECTED].** Gemini: the photo jumps between full-bleed, inset-sharp, and inset-rounded containers, snapping/resizing between steps. *Correction:* full-bleed photos on the working screens (Analysing, Result, Touch up, History Detail) are **intentional** — the app's founding design rule is "the photo is the app," and fitted-photo framing is settled (direction D13). Do NOT force one container on every screen, and do not round the working screens' photos. *Deliverable (the real defect, kept):* inside one clean flow (Analyse → Proposal → Processing → Result), the photo's fitted size must not visibly snap or resize between steps — keep the fitted rectangle consistent across those transitions. Home's demo card keeps its framed card treatment.

**G1.2 Exit/back placement.** As Gemini wrote: the exit/back action sits top-left on Proposal, top-right on the Paywall, floating circular on Result (top-left) and History Detail (conflicting back arrow + floating X), and Brush has none (bottom text Cancel). Standardise per Gemini: exit/back top-left everywhere; top-right reserved for secondary actions; one treatment (chrome button) for the floating variant. (Brush's own dock anatomy from the shipped S5 review stays — give Brush the standard top-left exit treatment as part of this item, keeping its dock as built.)

**G1.3 Compare slider unification.** As Gemini wrote: one slider component app-wide — Home's handle/labels (Original / Cleaned) vs Result/History Detail (Before / After) differ, and the History Detail divider line bleeds outside the photo into the padding. Unify the component and labels; fix the line bleed (the divider must end at the photo's edges).

**G1.4 History badge inconsistency.** As Gemini wrote: Home tray thumbnails show a check-only pill; History grid thumbnails show dot + "Cleaned" pill — same data, two badges. Make the shared thumbnail show one identical badge in both places.

**G1.5 Secondary button & colour mismatches.** As Gemini wrote: Home's "See all" link is an off-palette teal (brand is #2563EB) — unify links to primary; Result's secondary buttons and the Saved state use mismatched treatments; History Detail's Share (outlined) vs Delete (ghost) sit inconsistently side by side — give side-by-side actions consistent heights/containers.

**G2.1 Paywall prices [CORRECTED].** Gemini flags missing prices as a critical blocker. *Correction:* prices are absent **by design, for now** — the store connection (RevenueCat) is not wired yet, so `PurchaseService` returns no prices and S8 honestly shows its prices-unavailable state (built to the S8 brief). There is **no app-code fix for this item today**; it resolves when the store wiring lands (a later phase, not this plan). Do NOT hard-code prices, do NOT add per-month breakdowns or "Save X%" badges — store prices arrive as localised strings only; nothing may be derived from them. Terms/Privacy links (also Gemini §3.6): **flag only** — link targets are a product decision; do not invent URLs or link rows without targets.

**G2.4 Double compare icon.** As Gemini wrote: Result shows the ⇄ icon twice (slider handle + top-right corner); History Detail stacks handle + X + "Tap icon to compare." Remove the duplicate corner icon on Result; on History Detail keep ONE compare affordance. The centre slider stays draggable everywhere. (Hold-to-compare already exists — see G4.4.)

**G2.5 Fix with brush disappears after save.** Gemini: after Save, "Fix with brush" vanishes, forcing a restart and raising a second-credit fear. *Execution order:* first VERIFY in the current build whether the button truly disappears post-save; report what you find. If true, fix as Gemini wrote: keep Fix with brush available after saving; a touch-up on the same photo updates the saved result (a touch-up consumes no save — saves are consumed only when a result is saved from S7; that is already the product rule).

**G2.6 Paywall entry point [CORRECTED].** Gemini: Home has no Settings gear and no Pro entry; users can't reach Pro until saves run out. *Correction:* the Settings entry is **commented out on purpose (owner's decision) — do not add a Settings icon, do not restore the entry.** The Pro half of the item stands: add the subtle **PRO pill** in Home's header (top-right) opening S8 (deliberate arrival already exists in the brief/build). The saves line stays as built.

## Phase 2 — Next UX sprint

**G2.2 + G3.2 Proposal screen, crowded photos [CORRECTED].** Gemini: with 18 people, the ovals are unreadable, the large cards force 6 pages of swiping, and the red "Remove" pill is ambiguous (state or action?). *Correction:* tapping to toggle **already exists** (canvas regions and cards both toggle — shipped in the Review-04 fix round); do not rebuild it. *Deliverables (Gemini's, kept):* (a) a compact horizontal strip of small circular person crops (subject first, kept-subject marked) alongside/under the photo, synced both ways with canvas taps — tapping a crop highlights that person on the photo; (b) the Remove/Keep pill must read unambiguously as a STATE with a tap to switch; (c) compress the overlap warning banner to a compact single-line pill so the photo gains height. Gemini's full mask-over-photo repaint (semi-transparent red segmentation instead of outlines) is NOT adopted as written — the outline treatment is the shipped, reviewed design; the strip + highlight carries the readability fix. If you believe the mask is required for the strip to work, STOP and flag it rather than repainting the canvas.

**G2.3 Brush zoom + loupe [CORRECTED].** Gemini: no pinch zoom/pan, no magnifier loupe, fingertip hides tiny targets; a large instruction banner covers the top of the photo. *Correction:* the instruction element is already a small chip inside the photo's fitted rect and it disappears after the first stroke (shipped, Review 07) — if your build shows a large banner, report the state you found before changing anything. *Deliverable (Gemini's, kept):* two-finger pinch-to-zoom/pan on the brush canvas and a floating magnifier loupe following the finger while painting. This is the biggest item in the plan (Medium–Large); it must not break the fitted-photo surface, stroke coordinates, or the dock's fixing state — if it cannot be done without those breaking, STOP and flag with what you found.

**G2.7 Processing status [CORRECTED].** Gemini: no status heading; "estimated seconds" proposed; Work in background should be demoted. *Corrections:* status/stage text **exists** on Processing (real stage labels ship today) — verify it is visible in every processing state (including the backgrounded/banner states); fix only a state that truly lacks it. "Estimated seconds" is **removed** — the app never shows time estimates; the server provides no honest one (settled honesty rule). Demoting Work in background is **removed** — its primary styling is a deliberate, owner-approved decision: teaching the user they may safely leave is that screen's job. Net deliverable = the visibility verification only.

## Phase 3 — Polish pass

**G3.1 Home thumb-zone + quota pill [CORRECTED].** Gemini: pin Pick + quota to a bottom dock; turn the saves line into a pill/card with a thin progress bar + Upgrade chevron; change the demo caption "Real app output" to editorial wording. *Correction:* the caption wording is settled copy — **out of scope, do not change any text.** *Deliverables (kept):* the bottom-dock placement of Pick + saves line, and the saves line presented as a surface pill that opens the paywall (its tap behaviour already exists at 0 saves; with the G2.6 PRO pill, deliberate opening exists too). The thin progress bar inside the pill: build only if it reads as the saves count it represents (10-segment or proportional fill of REMAINING saves — a factual meter, labelled by the existing line text); if it would need new copy, flag it.

**G3.3 Result action hierarchy [CORRECTED].** Gemini: separate editing actions (Touch up, Edit people) into a pill bar under the photo, output actions (Save, Share) at the bottom; add quota micro-copy under Save ("Uses 1 free save · 8 left"). *Correction:* the micro-copy is **removed** — the Result's fact lines (summary/saves/location) were deliberately removed by the owner himself; do not restore them in any form. *Deliverable (kept):* the two-group action hierarchy (editing bar vs output row), using the buttons and labels that already exist.

**G3.4 Brush floating controls.** Gemini: move Undo/Clear into floating pills on the canvas; live brush-size circle preview in the centre of the photo while the size slider moves. *Execution note (fact):* a size preview dot already exists in the size row (shipped, Review 07) — this item extends it onto the canvas as Gemini describes; keep the row preview too. The top-left exit comes from G1.2; do not also move Cancel out of the dock in this item.

**G3.5 History grid + re-save [CORRECTED].** Gemini: full-width 2-column grid (thumbnails currently leave a blank right column); a retention subtitle ("Stored locally on your device for 30 days"); a Save to Gallery button on History Detail. *Correction:* the 30-day line is **removed — it is an invented fact** (on-device history has no 30-day limit; never ship a retention claim that isn't a product rule). *Deliverables (kept):* the full-width 2-column grid, and Save to Gallery on History Detail (re-export the stored result to the device gallery).

**G2.8 + G3.6 Paywall value framing [CORRECTED].** Gemini: add 2–3 value bullets; the subtitle ("removes the monthly limit") contradicts "more saves"; add BEST VALUE · SAVE 60% and POPULAR pills; Terms/Privacy links. *Corrections:* Pro is **300 saves/month, not unlimited** — any bullet or framing that says "unlimited" is factually wrong and must not ship; the value facts are exactly: 300 saves a month · every save full resolution · no watermark (the last two are also true of free). "SAVE 60%" is **removed** (nothing may be derived from store price strings — see G2.1); a "Best value" badge on Annual already ships from the S8 brief — do not add a second badge system or a POPULAR pill. Subtitle-consistency: the shipped headline/subline are the brief's settled copy ("10 saves a month becomes 300") — if the built screen still shows an older "removes the monthly limit" line anywhere, that is a FACT bug: report it, do not rewrite it yourself (copy comes from the design lead). Terms/Privacy links: flag only (see G2.1). *Net deliverable:* report-only on copy/prices; build nothing here except confirming no stale "unlimited"-style claim exists in the built screen.

**G4.1 Slider haptic ticks.** As Gemini wrote: a quiet haptic micro-tick as the compare slider's line passes each removed person's position, on Home demo / Result / History Detail. Use the existing `AppHaptics` vocabulary; ticks only while the user is actively dragging (never during the teaching sweep or any automatic motion).

**G4.3 Result sweep reveal.** As Gemini wrote: on Result arrival, the split starts mostly-Before and glides to mostly-After (~0.8 s, decelerating spring), settling centred. *Execution note:* this must compose with the existing springSettle dissolve — the arrival is ONE motion story, not two effects stacked; if the two fight, STOP and flag with a recording instead of shipping both.

**G4.2 Frosted processing silhouettes [REPLACED — not executable].** Gemini proposes per-person frosted/shimmer silhouettes pulsing during processing. *Fact:* the processing contract returns ONE finished photo; the app has no per-person progress to show, and per-person live preview was explicitly deferred for that reason. Animating silhouettes "as the AI reconstructs" would depict progress that is not happening — the app never fakes a state. **Do not build this.** It stays parked until a future pipeline contract provides real per-person data.

**G4.4 Long-press original peek [CORRECTED].** Gemini proposes press-and-hold to flash the Original on Result and History Detail. *Correction:* **this already ships** (hold-to-compare on Result: press-and-hold shows the original). Verify it also works on History Detail; if missing there, add it there only. The 99% scale-down detail is the only new element — optional micro-polish, last in this phase, skip it if anything above is unfinished.
