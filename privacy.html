Privacy Policy — Uncrowd

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Uncrowd is a photo app that removes background people from photos you choose. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how long it is kept. It applies to the Uncrowd Android app published by Artivo.

What the app does with your photos

  • You pick a photo using your phone's system photo picker. The app does not have access to your photo library — it only receives the single photo you choose.
  • To remove people, the photo is sent securely (encrypted in transit) to our processing server. Processing cannot happen on the phone itself, so this upload is required for the app to work.
  • Uploaded photos and processed results are stored on the server only as long as needed to complete and deliver your request, and are automatically deleted within 24 hours.
  • Cleaned photos saved to your phone have their location (GPS) data removed before saving.

Accounts

The app works with an anonymous account created on your device. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or password. Your anonymous account is used only to track your monthly free saves and any Pro entitlement, so the same limits apply fairly.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details. We receive confirmation from Google that a purchase is active so the app can unlock Pro features.

What we never do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or share your photos or personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Diagnostics

If the app runs into a problem, it keeps a short technical log on your device only. This log is never sent to us automatically. The app offers a "share diagnostics" option; only if you choose it is the log shared, using whatever app you pick to send it.

Service providers

We use secure cloud services (Supabase for the app backend and a processing server) purely to operate the app: storing your anonymous account record, delivering your cleaned photo, and enforcing the deletion described above. They process data on our instructions only.

Children

Uncrowd is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices and deletion

Photos are deleted automatically within 24 hours as described above. You can also request deletion of your anonymous account data at any time — see the account and data deletion page for how.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
